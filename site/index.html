The through line

Useful AI needs
good boundaries.

My approach is to make the input, responsibility, and evidence clear before asking a model to help.

Read the engineering principles ↗
  1. 01

    Start with the actual problem.

    Customer pain points and technical signals should lead to useful engineering action, not automation for its own sake.

  2. 02

    Make the contract inspectable.

    Scoped tools, deterministic checks, and traceable evidence make the system easier to trust—and easier to challenge.

  3. 03

    Keep judgment human.

    Agents draft and recommend. People own acceptance, disclosure, and publication.
